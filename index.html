Research profile

Yuyang Zhang

Materials modeller working across multiphysics simulation, sensing, and machine learning.

yuyangzhang2002@foxmail.com GitHub Personal website

My research connects computational materials science with physical sensing systems. I build coupled mechanical, electrostatic, transport, and reaction models, then use experiments and data-driven methods to test and extend their predictions.

Selected Research

2026

Multiphysics drug delivery

Simulation-enhanced design of hollow microneedles for convection-enhanced drug delivery

Collaborator: Tian Yuan, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Imperial College London

Constructed an integrated model coupling microneedle infusion, convection-diffusion transport within tumour tissue, and chemical reaction processes to analyse drug distribution in brain tumours.

2025

Machine vision

Machine vision and sensor development for early fungi detection

Advisor: Christoper Blanford, Department of Materials, The University of Manchester

Built a spore-monitoring prototype with Raspberry Pi and an Arducam camera, then trained machine-learning models on artificially incubated spore images. The work formed part of the SONY UK Sentinel project for agricultural and medical sensing.

2024

Ionic logic

Sensor and ionic logic-gate modelling

Advisor: Di Wei, Beijing Institute of Nanoenergy and Nanosystems

Developed COMSOL models that enabled direct comparison between simulation and experiment. Incorporated code-generated randomised structures to reproduce geometries used in experimental devices.

2023

Triboelectric sensing

Contact-separation triboelectric nanogenerator model

Advisor: Jiajia Shao, Beijing Institute of Nanoenergy and Nanosystems

Proposed a coupled mechanical-electrostatic model for contact-separation mode triboelectric nanogenerators with Bobo Sun. The model supported subsequent momentum-transfer analysis with Zeyang Yu and was later developed into a web-backed application.
